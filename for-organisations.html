What the pattern can do

Useful before it is impressive.

Answer routine questions

Give people clear, consistent answers from your approved information without making them hunt through pages and PDFs.

Check changing information

For suitable use cases, current-source retrieval can verify information that changes, then show users where the answer came from.

Hand off when needed

Route people to official pages, forms, staff, phone numbers or enquiry capture when the assistant should not be the final authority.
