Privacy information

How Ask Wakefield handles data

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the chat. Ask Wakefield is designed for local information, not private financial, medical, legal or account-specific matters.

Who operates Ask Wakefield?

Ask Wakefield is an independent service operated by Mediahubink Limited, 7 Tileyard North, Wakefield WF1 5FY. It is not an official Wakefield Council service. Privacy enquiries can be sent to hello@mediahubink.com.

Chat messages

When you send a message, the conversation content needed to answer your question is sent to an AI service provider. Ask Wakefield does not provide a user account or a saved chat-history feature. Technical logs and provider-side retention may still apply in accordance with the service provider's settings and policies.

For questions that depend on changing information, the AI service may use web search to retrieve current information from selected sources.

Event submissions

If you submit an event, we collect the event information you provide together with your name, email address and optional organisation details. We use this information to review the listing, contact you about the submission, and manage the local event information supplied to Ask Wakefield.

Event submissions are processed using services including Google Sheets and email delivery infrastructure configured by Mediahubink.

Technical information

Our hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical information required to serve and protect the site, such as request timestamps, IP addresses, browser information and server logs. We also use basic request controls to reduce abuse of the chat service.

What we ask you not to submit

  • Passwords or authentication codes
  • Bank or card details
  • Medical or highly sensitive personal information
  • Confidential case details intended for Wakefield Council or another public authority

Your rights and questions

If you have a question about personal information you submitted directly to Mediahubink through Ask Wakefield, or want to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, contact hello@mediahubink.com. You can also raise data-protection concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this notice

We may update this page when the service, providers or data flows change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
